Overview

This app is your single source of truth for tracking the Vermont state contract. It replaces the two Excel spreadsheets you've been using by storing weekly cost entries, computing billing automatically, generating invoices at $145/hr, and showing you exactly how much you're keeping as the owner after taxes.

How It All Works Together

Everything flows from a single source: Weekly Cost Entries. When you add entries, every other number in the app updates automatically — the dashboard chart, billing totals, invoice amounts, and profit calculations all derive from those entries.

Key Principle
The contract max is $209,000 billed to the State of Vermont at $145/hr. Owner Draw = $209,000 − total ops costs. That margin is distributed proportionally across each week and added to the week's ops cost to get the billable amount.

◫ Weekly Costs New

This is the primary data entry screen. It mirrors the "By Week Costs" tab in your Excel spreadsheet and is the ground truth for all financial calculations.

Top stat cards

  • Total Ops Cost — all subcontractor + direct costs entered so far
  • Owner Draw — Contract Value ($209K) minus Total Ops Cost
  • Contract Value — the $209K ceiling
  • Total Weeks — number of distinct weeks that have entries

Weekly summary table

Each row represents one work week. Click a row to load that week's individual entries in the panel below. The table shows:

ColumnMeaning
WeekWeek number (e.g. Week 1, Week 2)
DatesThe week_start and week_end dates you entered
Ops CostTotal of all line costs for that week (labor + flat fees + direct costs)
+ MarginThis week's share of the owner draw, allocated proportionally
= BillableOps Cost + Margin = what gets billed to VT for this week
Hours @ $145Billable ÷ $145 = equivalent hours on the State invoice
Proportional Allocation
If total ops cost is $50,000 and one week's ops cost is $5,000 (10% of total), that week gets 10% of the owner draw added as margin. This keeps billing proportional to actual activity.

Adding an entry

1
Click + Add Entry (top right of the entries panel)
2
Fill in the form:
  • Person — who did the work (Ben, Garra, Joelle, Meagan, VT Consultant, COST, Owner)
  • Task Code — which contract task (A through H, or OH for Overhead)
  • Major Task — the specific deliverable (e.g. "Network Mtg #1 (May)")
  • Entry Type — see Entry Types section below
  • Week Number — which week of the contract (1, 2, 3…)
  • Week Start / End — calendar dates for that week
3
If Hourly Labor: enter Hours and Hourly Rate ($). A live preview shows the line cost as you type.

If any other type: enter a single Flat Amount ($).
4
Click Save. The weekly summary table updates immediately.

Editing or deleting an entry

Click any row in the entries table to open the edit modal. Make changes and click Save, or click the red Delete button to remove the entry entirely.

◪ Task Allocations New

This page tracks how much each subcontractor should be paid per task per month, and generates a Payout Report showing exactly what to send each person.

Payout Report

Select a month at the top of the page to see a breakdown by vendor showing:

  • Which task(s) they worked on
  • Their percentage of that task's total payment
  • Their dollar amount for that task that month
  • A vendor total — the check amount to write

Adding an allocation

Click + Add Allocation and fill in:

  • Vendor — the subcontractor (must exist in the Vendors page first)
  • Major Task Name — the task category
  • Total Task Payment — total amount budgeted for this task overall
  • Month — which month (YYYY-MM format)
  • Month Amount — how much of that total is allocated this month
  • % of Task — this vendor's percentage share (e.g. 0.50 for 50%)

◬ Owner Profit New

Track your monthly take-home after paying subcontractors and covering expenses. The app automatically sets aside 40% for taxes so you always know what's truly available.

Summary stat cards (appear once you have records)

  • Total Income — sum of all gross income entries
  • Total Expenses — sub costs + direct + indirect combined
  • Owner Profit — income minus all expenses
  • Tax Set-Aside — 40% of owner profit, reserved for taxes
  • Net After Tax — what you actually keep

Adding a month

1
Click + Add Month and select the month.
2
Click ⚡ Auto-Compute to pull actuals automatically:
  • Gross income is pulled from Client Invoices for that month
  • Subcontractor costs are pulled from hourly_labor and flat_fee Weekly Entries
  • Direct expenses are pulled from direct_cost and travel_stipend entries
3
Review or adjust the numbers. The live preview panel shows your owner profit, tax set-aside, and net after tax as you type — before saving.
4
Click Save. If a record for that month already exists, it is updated (no duplicate entries).
Owner Profit = Gross Income − Subcontractor Costs − Direct Expenses − Indirect Expenses Tax Set-Aside = Owner Profit × 40% Net After Tax = Owner Profit − Tax Set-Aside
Indirect Expenses
Indirect expenses (overhead that isn't directly billable) default to $0. Add them manually if you have costs like software subscriptions or home office expenses that should reduce your profit.

Which costs count toward subcontractor costs in Owner Profit?
Auto-Compute treats hourly_labor and flat_fee entries as subcontractor costs. direct_cost and travel_stipend entries are treated as direct expenses.

Formulas & Calculations

Line cost (per entry)

Hourly labor: Hours × Hourly Rate All other types: Flat Amount

Weekly ops cost

Week Ops Cost = sum of all line costs for entries in that week

Owner draw (contract-level)

Owner Draw = Contract Value ($209,000) − Total Ops Cost (all weeks)

Weekly margin allocation

Week Margin = (Week Ops Cost ÷ Total Ops Cost) × Owner Draw

Weekly billable

Billable = Week Ops Cost + Week Margin

Billing rate conversion

Hours to Invoice = Billable ÷ $145

Monthly billing (for invoice generation)

Weeks are attributed to the month of their week_start date. Monthly Billable = sum of Billable for all weeks starting in that month.

Owner profit

Owner Profit = Gross Income − Sub Costs − Direct Expenses − Indirect Expenses Tax Set-Aside = Owner Profit × Tax Rate (default 40%) Net After Tax = Owner Profit − Tax Set-Aside

Recommended Weekly/Monthly Workflow

Every week (as work happens)

1
Go to Weekly Costs. Click + Add Entry for each person/task combination that had activity this week.
2
Make sure each entry has the correct Week Number and Week Start/End dates. The week number should increment each week (Week 1, Week 2, …).
3
Verify the weekly summary row looks right — ops cost should match what you paid out, and billable should be higher (ops + margin).

End of each month

1
Go to Client Invoices. Under Invoice Generator, select the month and click Calculate Invoice. Review the task-code breakdown.
2
Click → Create Invoice Draft, fill in the invoice number, set status to Sent, and save.
3
Go to Task Allocations. Add allocations for each subcontractor for the month, then check the Payout Report to see what to pay each person.
4
Go to Owner Profit. Click + Add Month, select the month, and click ⚡ Auto-Compute. Review the preview (profit, tax set-aside, net), then Save.
5
When VT pays the invoice, go back to Client Invoices, click the invoice row, and click Record Payment.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if a week spans two months?

The entire week is attributed to the month of its week_start date. For example, a week starting January 29 and ending February 4 counts as a January week for billing purposes. If this causes an issue, split it into two entries with different week numbers.

Can I change the tax rate from 40%?

Yes. When adding or editing an Owner Profit record, change the Tax Rate field (e.g. enter 0.35 for 35%). The live preview updates immediately.

What if I haven't entered any weekly entries yet?

The Dashboard chart and billing tables won't show data until you have at least a few weekly entries. The dashboard will fall back to showing Billed to Date from Client Invoices. Start by entering a couple of weeks of history from your Excel spreadsheet.

Do I need to enter Task Allocations for the Invoice Generator to work?

No. The Invoice Generator reads from Weekly Entries only, not Task Allocations. Task Allocations are used solely for the subcontractor Payout Report — what you owe each person.

The Owner Profit auto-compute shows $0 for gross income. Why?

Auto-Compute pulls gross income from Client Invoices with a date in the selected month. If you haven't created a client invoice for that month yet, income will be $0. Generate the invoice first (or enter it manually), then run auto-compute.

Can I have more than one contract?

The app currently loads the first contract in the system for all financial calculations. If you need to track a second contract, it would require a Settings update to support contract switching in the nav.
